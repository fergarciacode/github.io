Work experience
  • Jul 2022 – Dec 2025 LABEC

    Research Fellowship – AI Development

    Istituto Nazionale di Fisica Nucleare, Sezione di Firenze · Florence, Italy

    The work involved designing and training Deep Neural Networks for the automatic recoloring of pictorial artworks from raw nuclear imaging data obtained through XRF. In parallel, I developed a Python package for XRF spectra simulation and built a Dash-based web application to interactively visualize and explore its functionality. This multi-faceted project required the integration of deep learning, scientific computing, software engineering, web development, and containerized deployment workflows (Docker), significantly strengthening my professional expertise in AI and IT infrastructure.

  • Feb 2022 – Jun 2022 CALSENS © Valencia, Spain

    Mathematics Advisor

    I developed a Python code for measuring the deformation of civil structures. The code utilized the Frenet-Serret equations based on curvature measures obtained through optical devices placed on the structures.
